# Exagent video rewrite

## Purpose

Contrast the crowded, reactive experience of applying with a proactive way to discover and approach opportunities. The product demonstration is the centre of the film: it must show the real Exagent interface, not a conceptual mock-up.

## Narrative arc

1. **Logo (0:00–0:02)**
   A brief Exagent logo reveal. Keep it clean and move straight into the story.

2. **Sea of applicants (0:02–0:08)**
   Use a sea visualisation style. One applicant is surrounded by countless other applicants, and is difficult to distinguish when viewed from afar, regardless of what they do. This is not a queue: it is a picture of how hard it is to be seen among a vast number of people.

   Suggested copy: “One applicant among thousands.”

   Optional voiceover: “When everyone applies, it is hard to be seen.”

3. **Forest / opportunity landscape (0:08–0:13)**
   Transition from the sea to a dense, unmapped landscape of companies and possible opportunities. The opportunity is not yet visible or reachable.

   Suggested copy: “The opportunity may be elsewhere.”

4. **Manual friction (0:13–0:18)**
   Show the current proactive approach: searching for companies, opening many tabs, researching people, comparing notes, and deciding whom to contact. It is possible, but laborious.

   Suggested copy: “Finding it takes work.”

5. **Solution and real product flow (0:18–0:42)**
   Move into one continuous screen recording of the real Exagent app. Explain the pipeline as the viewer watches each step happen; do not put generic explainer animation over unrelated product UI.

   | Time | Product moment | Pipeline label and message |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | 0:18–0:22 | Set **Area**, **Industry**, and **Company size**, then search. | **1. Set your target** — “Start with where you want to work, the industry, and the company size.” |
   | 0:22–0:26 | Show the selected filters, loading state, and relevant company cards. | **2. Find relevant companies** — “Exagent finds companies that fit your search.” |
   | 0:26–0:30 | Show the `Finding key people` progress state and a company’s key-people list. | **3. Find the people who matter** — “Then it identifies relevant people at those companies.” |
   | 0:30–0:35 | Select a person and open their public-background details. | **4. Add useful context** — “Open a profile to see the public context behind the person.” |
   | 0:35–0:42 | Add the user’s information in **About you**, then show refreshed ranked people, score, and reasoning. | **5. Prioritise your best opportunities** — “Exagent helps you focus on the contacts most worth pursuing for you.” |

   Suggested voiceover:

   > Start with where you want to work, the industry, and the kind of company you’re looking for. Exagent finds relevant companies and the people who matter inside them. Open a profile for public context, then add your own background to see the opportunities most worth pursuing—ranked for you.

6. **Outcome (0:42–0:48)**
   Hold on the ranked list or a selected contact. The clutter has resolved into a clear next step.

   Suggested copy: “Find the companies worth exploring. Know who to contact first.”

7. **End card (0:48–0:53)**
   Exagent logo, the final line, QR code, and website. Leave the card on screen for about 5–6 seconds so it can be scanned. The QR code should go directly to the app’s starting screen and be paired with a readable URL; do not make one dependent on the other. Add a social link only if it does not clutter the primary call to action.

## Production principles

- The applicant-sea scene conveys visibility and crowding, not waiting in a line.
- Keep the forest metaphor short; the literal interface should take precedence once Exagent appears.
- Record the actual product at a readable scale. Use edits or subtle callouts to guide attention rather than replacing the UI with a mock-up.
- Describe the ranking as a prioritisation aid, not as a guarantee of an outcome.
- The personal ranking becomes meaningful after the user provides their background in **About you**. Without it, avoid suggesting a personalised-fit score.
- Do not imply that Exagent finds open roles; it finds relevant companies and people.
